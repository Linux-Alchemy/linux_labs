# OS_Builds

There's things in them there hills fellas!

The `/OS_Builds` folder is a collection of custom Linux distro builds — some experimental, some practical, and some built purely for the hell of it. Think of it as **nerdcraft**: part hacking project, part creative outlet, and part seeing what I can do with a few cups of coffee and a dream (too dramatic?)

---

## What's the Point?

Sometimes these builds are minimal and clean. Other times they’re themed beyond recognition. A few might get hardened and prepped for actual live use — others are just weird, intentionally cursed, or hyper-specialized for a specific task (like pentesting, photo editing, or flipping the bird to Windows 11).

There’s no one formula here — just me building what I want, how I want.

---

## What to Expect

Each build will include:
- A themed and purpose-driven Arch or Debian-based system
- Clean documentation (3-phase style: base install, daily apps, security/hardening)
- Notes on pain points, cool discoveries, and config tweaks
- Occasional spice and attitude — because Linux should be fun, not corporate

You’ll find:
- Snapshots of custom desktops
- Toolkits tailored to the build’s theme
- Firejail and AppArmor setups for sandboxing
- UFW configs, Btrfs snapshots, flatpak security tweaks, and more

---

## Why Build Your Own?

Because it’s **satisfying as hell** to strip away the bloat and build something that’s fully yours.

Coming from years of Windows like many people, I find the freedom of Linux is addictive. No registry BS, no forced updates, no resource hogging — just something cool, the way you want it. Whether it’s building a daily driver, a live ISO, or something hilariously overengineered, **this repo is where I scratch that itch.**

---

## Who’s This For?

Me — but also anyone who:
- Likes the idea of building their own OS
- Wants to see how someone else tackles theming, hardening, and system setup
- Thinks Arch or Debian are cool but doesn’t want to read a 50-page wiki just to install Firefox
- Believes in owning your system, not renting it from Microsoft
